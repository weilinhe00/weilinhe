Dissertation
Integrating Multi-Omics Datasets to Build a Regulatory Network and Identify Molecular Drivers of Micronutrient Deficiency in Pregnancy

My MSc dissertation (supervised by Daniel D'Andrea). I integrated multi-omics datasets to construct a regulatory network and benchmarked feature-selection strategies against several learning algorithms to identify candidate molecular drivers of micronutrient deficiency in pregnancy.

Mini-Project · Group 28
Prediction of Non-Coding RNAs' Role in Cancer Through Multi-Omics Data Integration

A group project investigating the regulatory mechanism of lncRNA MIR100HG in cancer by integrating gene expression, DNA methylation, and transcription factor–target gene data into a reproducible multi-stage pipeline.

Group report (PDF) Contribution (PDF) Slides (PPTX)
EMATM0066
Visual Analytics
Visualising Housing Inequality: A Multi-Modal Dashboard for Structural Evolution and Affordability Prediction in England and Wales

An interactive Tableau dashboard integrating six 2011 Census housing tables with house-price and income data. I applied PCA and UMAP for dimensionality reduction to analyse the structural evolution of housing tenure and spatial disparities in affordability.

Report (PDF) Dashboard (Tableau) Demo video
EMATM0067
AI & Text Analytics
Semantic Clustering and Classification in Text Analysis

Constructed context-based word representations to study semantic proximity between content words in English novels, comparing KMeans, Agglomerative Clustering, and DBSCAN alongside graph analysis to uncover latent semantic structures.

Report (PDF)

AI & Text Analytics
A Comparative Analysis of Machine Learning Methods for Sentiment Classification and Topic Modeling in Climate Change Discourse

Enhanced a baseline Naive Bayes classifier and compared three models for sentiment classification, then applied topic modeling to climate-change discourse, analysing misclassified examples to surface model strengths and limitations.
